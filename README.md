# sentinels_data_analysis
An in-depth analysis of data, statistics, and performance by the Sentinels Professional Valorant Team.

Datasets created based on statistics from VCT Kickoff 2026: Americas, and VCT 2026: Americas Stage 1.

6 different datasets covering individual stats round-by-round, individual stats by the series, individual stats in comparison to other players, and team stats in comparison to other teams
